Questions parents ask

Is Compound Wahala free?

Yes. The whole game is free to play in your browser. There are no ads and nothing to buy.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Open the link and tap PLAY. It works in Chrome, Safari, Edge and Firefox, on phones and computers. You can add it to your home screen to open it like an app.

What age is it for?

It is made for children from about 7 and for families. Every level uses big buttons, short sentences, and wrong answers are funny instead of punished.

Is it safe for children?

There is no chat, no accounts, no ads and no purchases. Anonymous play statistics are only collected if you say yes, and you can delete them in Settings.

What language is the game in?

English with Nigerian Pidgin. Pidgin lines show a plain English version underneath, so players who don't speak Pidgin can follow along.

How long does it take?

Each of the 5 story levels takes a few minutes. After that, Full Week mode is a longer 7-day challenge with bills, blackouts and compound meetings.

Is it a true story?

No. All characters and places are fictional, and the character art is AI-generated. The problems are inspired by everyday life in Nigerian compounds.
