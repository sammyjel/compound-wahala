Privacy

Short version: Compound Wahala does not ask for your name, email, phone number or location. If you say yes to anonymous statistics, we count things like "a level was finished" to make the game better. You can say no, change your mind in Settings, or delete what was sent.

What stays on your device

Your game progress, coins, stars, settings and the name you type in Full Week mode are saved only in your browser (localStorage). They never leave your device. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

Anonymous play statistics (only if you say yes)

When you first open the game we ask. Nothing is sent before you answer, and nothing is sent if you choose No thanks.

We recordWhy
A random id made by your browser (not linked to you)To count returning players and retention
Game events: game opened, level started / finished, stars, wrong guesses, seconds played, decorations bought with game coinsTo find levels that are too hard, too long or confusing
Device type (phone, tablet or desktop) and game versionTo fix layout problems
Where you came from: the website name or a campaign tag in the link (for example utm_source=tiktok)To learn which posts bring players
Short error messages if the game crashesTo fix bugs

We do not record names, emails, IP addresses in our database, precise location, contacts or anything you type. We do not use advertising trackers and we do not sell data.

Your choices

  • Turn statistics on or off: Settings → Anonymous play statistics.
  • Delete your data: Settings → Delete my statistics data. This erases everything recorded for your random id on our server and gives you a new id.

Children

The game is made to be safe for young players: there are no chats, no accounts, no real-money purchases and no ads. Statistics are anonymous and optional.

Money

Coins are play money earned in the game. They cannot be bought, and the game asks for no payment details.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue on GitHub. Please do not post personal information there.
